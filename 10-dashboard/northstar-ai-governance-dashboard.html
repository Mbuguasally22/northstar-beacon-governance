How to read this page
  • All values are mock data. Only Beacon has real detail behind it in the case study. The other 21 systems are invented to fill the portfolio panels.
  • Green means inside the thresholds set for this dashboard. It does not mean safe. Read it next to control testing (B4) and incidents (C1).
  • The fairness ratio is a screening signal. It does not prove or rule out discrimination, and 0.80 is not a Canadian legal standard.
  • Thresholds are starting values that the AI Governance Committee would calibrate. Definitions are in the Dashboard Specification (NF-AIG-007) in the repository.
